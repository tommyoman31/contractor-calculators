About

No fluff, no dashboards you'll never open — just the numbers that show you exactly how profitable a job will be before you price it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work in Excel or Google Sheets?

Both. Every calculator is a standard .xlsx file, so it opens in Excel and works in Google Sheets too. No plug-ins or special software needed.

What if I mess up the formulas?

You can't. Every formula cell is locked, so you can type in your numbers without any risk of accidentally deleting or overwriting the math.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every calculator is a one-time purchase. Pay once, download the file, and it's yours to use for good.

Do I get future updates?

Yes. If a calculator gets improved, you get the updated file free — just redownload it from the same link. No repurchase needed.

What if I get stuck setting it up?

Every calculator comes with a step-by-step Start Here guide tab that walks you through setup. Still stuck? Reach out here and we'll help you sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

No. Every calculator's page shows exactly what you're getting before you buy — inputs, layout, and math included. Since it's an instant digital download, all sales are final.
